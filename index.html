Mulch Calculator — Cubic Yards, Bags & Depth

Enter your bed length, width and mulch depth to get cubic yards for a bulk delivery, plus the exact number of bags to buy at the store.

The arithmetic behind every answer

Length, width and depth are the only three numbers the formula needs. Depth goes in inches, so it is divided by 12 before it can be multiplied by anything else.

  • Cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
  • Bags of 2 cu ft = cubic feet ÷ 2, then rounded up to a whole bag

Nothing rounds down, because running short partway along a border is the expensive outcome, not the one spare bag left in the garage. Add roughly 10% for waste.

Worked example: a 20 ft × 10 ft bed at 3 inches

Twenty feet by ten feet is 200 square feet of planting space. At three inches deep the arithmetic is 20 × 10 × 3 ÷ 12 = 50 cubic feet. Divide 50 by 27 and you get 1.85 cubic yards, which is what goes on a bulk order. Divide 50 by 2 and you get 25 bags of 2 cu ft mulch; if the pallet only carries the larger bags, 50 ÷ 3 = 16.67, so you buy 17 bags of 3 cu ft.

Depth guidance

Extension services commonly advise no more than 2 to 3 inches of fine-textured mulch such as compost and bark on beds, and shallower depths for mulch around trees and shrubs, where a thick layer can hold moisture against the trunk. Check the local guidance before you pick a depth.
